How it works

From a sentence to sent — in three steps

No forms, no categories to hunt through. Tell Envoy what you're planning in plain language.

1

Describe your project

A wedding, a renovation, an offsite. Add your location and Envoy works out which vendors the project actually needs.

2

Get your lineup

A ranked shortlist by category, each with the reason that category matters for your project. Fine-tune it before anything goes out.

3

Envoy drafts. You approve.

Envoy writes each introduction in your voice, with your dates and details. Nothing sends until you say so.

Draft — introduction to a florist Illustrative example
To: Wildflower & Fern Studio From: you@yourdomain.com Subject: Outdoor wedding florals — October 12, 150 guests

Hi Sarah, We're getting married on October 12th just outside Austin — an outdoor ceremony and reception for about 150 guests. We're after garden-style arrangements in a muted autumn palette. Do you have availability that date, and could you share pricing for ceremony florals plus fifteen tables? Thanks so much, Maya & James

Awaiting your approval

You're in control. Every message sends from your address only after you approve it — so vendors reply to you, not to a marketplace relay.
