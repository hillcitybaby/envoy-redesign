Direction H · Overlay study

Full-bleed image, card overlaid

A close structural study of the reference you sent. Full-bleed image with a white card overlaid on the left third; floating UI sits on the image rather than beside it. The About section overlaps the hero with a large rounded notch, and the feature cards use corner cut-outs with the titles set inside them, alternating top and bottom.

The interaction

Hovering a project in the translucent picker swaps the image, the lineup, the pipeline numbers and the draft card together. Works on hover, click, focus and arrow keys.

Section map

Hero (value prop + the three case studies) → About (fuller value prop, coloured emphasis words) → Features (four things Envoy does) → the project input → Pricing → For business. The last two are ported from E and retoned to H's palette.

Wins

  • +Still compact — 4,579px with all six sections, vs 7,600–8,600 in Round 1
  • +Case studies earn their place by driving the whole hero
  • +The cut-out and notch details give it real craft identity

Gives up

  • −Primary action is below the fold — hero CTA only scrolls
  • −Leans hardest of all directions on photography it doesn't have
  • −Hover-only discovery is invisible on touch until tapped

Direction D · Glass

Deep sky & glassmorphism

Full-bleed cool gradient sky with a heavy scrim, floating glass pill nav, and a bento grid of case cards. The headline sets vendor categories as chips inline in the type — skysavvy's move, but carrying real product meaning rather than decoration.

Palette & type

Deep navy → cyan → cobalt. Bricolage Grotesque display over Instrument Sans.

Strongest

The hero. The inline category chips explain the whole product in one line, and the floating lineup card proves it without a word.

Weakest

The bento gets busy — five surfaces of different weights competing in one grid.

Wins

  • +Boldest hero of the three; strongest first impression
  • +Keeps a clear blue thread to the current brand
  • +Glass and depth read as premium, not trendy

Gives up

  • −Dark hero → light page is a hard transition
  • −Heaviest scrim work; least forgiving if photos get added
  • −Closest to the old site's coldness

Direction E · Mesh

Organic gradient mesh

Near-white page under a large blurred colour field — violet, cyan, mint, indigo. Centred hero with the composer as the single focal object, then an asymmetric case-study pair and a full-width draft strip. This is the Crucible and moana territory, in cool tones.

Palette & type

White ground, violet #5B45E0, cyan and mint accents. Instrument Sans throughout, 500 weight display with tight tracking.

Strongest

The hero mesh and composer. It looks like a 2026 AI product without saying "AI" once, and the text box is unmistakably the thing to use.

Weakest

The case studies lean on the big lineup panel to carry them; the two side tiles are thin by comparison.

Wins

  • +Most contemporary — reads as a current product, not a template
  • +Composer-as-hero makes the primary action unmissable
  • +Light ground matches the real app
  • +Warm without any taupe, terracotta or red

Gives up

  • −Violet mesh is the most common AI-product look right now
  • −Furthest from the existing blue
  • −Mesh needs care at small sizes or it turns to mush

Direction F · Inset

Inset shell & pill system

The whole page sits inside a rounded white shell floating on a grey ground — Evolve Bio's device. Pill-shaped brand and nav, floating stat pills under the hero, tile grid for cases, and expandable rows for the week-five / week-eight story. An icon set inline in the H1.

Palette & type

Grey ground, white shell, cobalt #1B47E0 and mint #00A87E. Instrument Sans throughout.

Strongest

The hero split — headline and composer left, full reasoned lineup panel right. You see the promise and the proof in one viewport, no scrolling.

Weakest

The most conventional of the three. The inset shell is the only genuinely unusual move.

Wins

  • +Clearest information design — easiest to scan and extend
  • +Stat pills give an honest home for real numbers later
  • +Cobalt keeps the brand blue while feeling new
  • +Safest for the founder to say yes to

Gives up

  • −Least bold — closest to a well-made default
  • −Inset shell costs horizontal room on small screens
  • −Mint + cobalt can read fintech if pushed further

Shared across D, E and F: four sections only — hero, case studies, pricing, for business. Hero always holds the text entry. Mark and wordmark kept; everything else new. No taupe, terracotta or red anywhere.

Integrity: no invented proof. Every placeholder is visibly labelled, all product UI is rebuilt HTML marked Illustrative example, email only — no SMS or calls — and real pricing.

Photography: all visuals are CSS/SVG-native. I could not source licence-clear stock at the quality these references set, so every photo slot is sized and labelled, ready to receive real images.

Known gaps: header nav collapses on mobile with no menu yet; contrast on gradient-backed text was verified by calculation rather than by the automated checker, which does not render backdrop-filter in this environment.
